## Places, by area

### Financial District

- **Black Fox Coffee · Pine Street**: Coffee · Café · roasting: Both · Multi-roaster
  - 70 Pine St, New York, NY 10005
  - Weekdays 7:00–17:00, weekends 8:00–16:00
  - The Black Fox with seats: a 1,500-square-foot café in a 1930s Art Deco tower that was once downtown's tallest. A true multi-roaster, it pours its own house blend beside guest coffees from roasters like Heart, 49th Parallel and George Howell, so the menu changes between visits. Get the flat white, and expect to wait for a table at the weekday rush.

### Tribeca

- **Interlude Coffee & Tea · Hudson**: Coffee · Café
  - 145 Hudson St, New York, NY 10013
  - Weekdays 7:00–16:00, weekends 8:00–15:00
  - A minimalist Tribeca loft of a café for coffee, matcha and tea, with pastries baked in-house. The matcha tonic and the malted chocolate chip cookie are the orders. A few tables inside and more out front; most people take it to go.

### SoHo & Nolita

- **Café Integral**: Coffee · Café · Pour-over
  - 149 Elizabeth St, New York, NY 10012
  - Daily 8:00–16:00
  - A 20-seat Nolita café founded in 2012 to champion Nicaraguan coffee, now pouring a rotating range that includes a Nicaraguan Geisha. Try the Japanese slow-drip or the cold brew on tap, and take it white with one of their house-made nut milks.
- **Torrisi** (food): Italian (new food kind) · reservation: Optional by policy; Required recommended
  - 275 Mulberry St, New York, NY 10012
  - Lunch Wed–Sat 11:30–14:00; dinner Mon–Sat 17:00–23:00; closed Sundays
  - Major Food Group's Michelin-starred Italian-American dining room in the landmark Puck Building, a fine-dining love letter to old Little Italy. Order the Italian and American hams, the tortellini and the Dover sole Francese, and finish with the affogato served in an oversized martini glass. Tables open on Resy 30 days ahead and go fast, walk-ins can try the bar counter, athletic wear is turned away, and it's a splurge.

### Chinatown

- **Fasan Coffee**: Coffee · Café · roasting: Guest roasters · Multi-roaster
  - 34 Allen St, New York, NY 10002
  - Thu–Sun 10:00–17:00 *(unverified)*
  - A narrow shop full of vintage furniture and antiques, some of them for sale, pouring guest coffees from roasters as far off as Berlin's Sandbox. Order the espresso cream cold brew, the yuzu bright brew or the red bean latte, then take a hoop-back chair out to the kerb.

### West Village

- **Arcane Estate Coffee**: Coffee · Café
  - 37 Cornelia St, New York, NY 10014
  - Wed–Fri 7:00–15:00, Sat–Sun 8:00–16:00; closed Mon–Tue *(unverified)*
  - A tiny, moody West Village café pouring only Panamanian coffee, almost all of it from founder Edgar Acosta-Masferrer's family land in the Chiriquí highlands, Geisha among it. It ranked No. 12 on the 2026 World's 100 Best Coffee Shops list, so go early: lines form, and the limited bean releases sell out, some capped at two bags a person. Order whichever single lot is pouring and drink it black, like a small tasting.
- **Partners Coffee · West Village**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house
  - 44 Charles St, New York, NY 10014
  - Daily 6:30–18:00 *(unverified)*
  - The Brooklyn roaster's West Village café, a sunlit room of exposed beams and brick where people settle in at the tables, with coffee roasted daily in Brooklyn. An easy sit-down stop on a West Village walk.
- **Joe Coffee Company · Waverly Place**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house
  - 141 Waverly Pl, New York, NY 10014
  - Daily 7:00–19:00
  - The first Joe, opened in 2003 by the Rubinstein family in a former dry cleaner, and still a neighbourhood regular's spot. A handful of seats inside and two benches on the patio out front; a classic New York espresso stop a few blocks from Washington Square Park.

### East Village

- **Abraço**: Coffee · Café · Espresso; Food & sweets
  - 81 E 7th St, New York, NY 10003
  - Mon–Thu 8:00–20:00, Fri–Sat 8:00–22:00, Sun 9:00–18:00 *(unverified)*
  - An East Village espresso institution since 2007, now in a bigger room across the street from its original hole in the wall, with a front patio and vinyl on the stereo. Get an espresso and a slice of the olive oil cake, which is always on; the Sparkling Cream Espresso is the other signature. Cash only, and there are no non-dairy milks.
- **La Cabra · East Village**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house · Light roast / Nordic; Food & sweets
  - 152 2nd Ave, New York, NY 10003
  - Weekdays 7:00–18:00, weekends 8:00–18:00
  - The Danish roaster's East Village café, and still the one with the line: Nordic light roasts, floral cold brews and a counter of sourdough and hand-laminated pastries. Order a pour-over and the cardamom bun. Seating is a few small tables on the sidewalk, and there are no reservations.
- **Hi-Collar**: Coffee · Kissaten · Settle in
  - 231 E 9th St, New York, NY 10003
  - Daily 11:30–16:00
  - A Japanese kissaten by day: siphon coffee brewed at the counter, with omurice, hayashi rice, thick hot cakes, coffee jelly and purin. Book on Resy for Thursday to Sunday; Monday to Wednesday is walk-in only, and last orders are half an hour before closing. In the evening the same address is Five Spot Jazz.
- **Coffee Project New York · East Village**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house · Pour-over
  - 239 E 5th St, New York, NY 10003
  - Weekdays 7:30–17:00, Sat 8:00–17:30, Sun 8:00–17:00 *(unverified)*
  - The original shop of a team that also runs an SCA training campus and roastery in Long Island City. Order the deconstructed latte, which arrives in its parts so you can taste each, or one of the pour-overs.
- **Kimura** (food): Motsunabe; Sukiyaki; Shabu-shabu (Hot pot) · reservation: Optional
  - 31 St Marks Pl, New York, NY 10003
  - Mon–Thu and Sun 12:00–23:30; Fri–Sat 12:00–01:00
  - A small East Village hot-pot house built around Hakata-style motsunabe, with sukiyaki, shabu-shabu and sushi besides. Order the collagen nabe, its pork-trotter broth stewed for hours until rich and almost milky. It's busy even on weeknights, so book on Resy.

### Chelsea & Hudson Yards

- **Black Fox Coffee · Manhattan West (Pendry)**: Coffee · Stand · roasting: Both · Grab & go · Multi-roaster
  - 438 W 33rd St (Pendry Manhattan West), New York, NY 10001
  - Daily 7:00–17:00
  - A long, narrow espresso counter with its own street door beside the Pendry hotel lobby, a short walk west of Moynihan Train Hall. Espresso drinks use the house All Day blend and the batch brew pours two coffees from a rotating guest roaster; pastries like the kouign-amann sit on the counter. No seats inside, just two small tables out front, and cups are takeaway only, so bring your own if you have one.
- **Intelligentsia · High Line Hotel**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house · Pour-over
  - 180 10th Ave (at W 20th St), New York, NY 10011
  - Daily 7:00–17:00
  - The Chicago roaster's only New York coffee bar, in the lobby of the High Line Hotel, a Gothic Revival former seminary. Order a pour-over or the Avena latte at the zinc-topped bar and take it out to the courtyard garden; the High Line is a block away.

### Flatiron & NoMad

- **Felix Roasting Co. · NoMad**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house
  - 450 Park Ave S, New York, NY 10016
  - Daily 8:00–17:00
  - Felix's 2018 flagship is as much an installation as a café: a hand-drawn mural of 500 tiles, a bespoke terrazzo floor and takeaway cups patterned to match. Come for the signature drinks made with house nut milks and syrups, like the hickory-smoked s'mores latte or the deconstructed espresso tonic.

### Hell's Kitchen

- **St Kilda Coffee · West 46th Street**: Coffee · Café · roasting: Both · Multi-roaster
  - 374 W 46th St, New York, NY 10036
  - Mon 7:00–18:00, Tue–Fri 7:00–20:00, Sat 8:00–20:00, Sun 8:00–18:00
  - A Melbourne-style café on Hell's Kitchen's Restaurant Row, roasting its own coffee and pouring rotating guest roasters beside it. Order a flat white; it's a good stop before a Broadway show, a couple of avenues west of Times Square. The smaller second shop on 44th Street has steps at the door.

### Midtown

- **Black Fox Coffee · 550 Madison (Garden kiosk)**: Coffee · Stand · roasting: Both · Grab & go · Multi-roaster
  - 550 Madison Ave, Garden Kiosk (between 55th & 56th Sts), New York, NY 10022
  - Weekdays 7:00–17:00, weekends 9:00–16:00
  - A takeaway kiosk in the public garden behind the former AT&T Building on Madison, with outdoor tables to sit at: a calm break a block from the Fifth Avenue crowds.
- **Black Fox Coffee · East 45th Street**: Coffee · Stand · roasting: Both · Grab & go · Multi-roaster
  - 45 E 45th St, New York, NY 10017
  - Weekdays 7:30–17:00, weekends 9:00–16:00
  - Black Fox's grab-and-go counter a block from Grand Central, handy before a train or between Midtown sights. Order a flat white and keep walking; the Pine Street branch is the one with seats.
- **WatchHouse · Fifth Avenue**: Coffee · Café
  - 660 5th Ave, New York, NY 10103
  - Weekdays 6:30–18:30, weekends 8:00–18:30
  - The London coffee house's Fifth Avenue café, steps from MoMA and a short walk from Rockefeller Center, with its own viennoiserie counter and tables outside. A polished stop for an espresso and a pastry between Midtown sights.
- **Culture Espresso · Bryant Park**: Coffee · Café · Espresso
  - 72 W 38th St, New York, NY 10018
  - Daily 7:00–19:00
  - A Midtown espresso bar since 2009, a block from Bryant Park, and a reliable good cup in a neighbourhood of chains. The chocolate chip cookie has a cult following; get one with an espresso or a flat white.

### Upper West Side

- **Irving Farm Coffee Roasters · Upper West Side**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house
  - 224 W 79th St, New York, NY 10024
  - Weekdays 7:00–18:00, weekends 7:30–18:00
  - The Upper West Side café of Irving Farm, one of New York's long-standing independent roasters, a few blocks from the Natural History Museum. A dependable cup before the museum or a walk into Central Park.

### Upper East Side

- **Oslo Coffee Roasters · Yorkville**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house · Grab & go
  - 422 E 75th St, New York, NY 10021
  - Weekdays 7:00–17:00, weekends 8:00–17:00
  - A small Upper East Side outpost of the Williamsburg roaster, on a quiet, tree-lined block. The room is tiny, so take your coffee to go and walk it toward the East River.

### Harlem

- **Superhet**: Coffee · roasting: Both · Multi-roaster; Pour-over
  - 390 Manhattan Ave, New York, NY 10026
  - Mon–Tue 7:00–13:00, Wed–Fri 7:00–15:00, Sat–Sun 9:00–15:00
  - Manny Amaro Jr.'s tiny counter, with metal on the stereo and band posters on the walls, pours a rotating cast of guest roasters beside his own small-batch roasts. The pour-over is the order, though the drip is excellent too. Morningside Park is a block away.

### Greenpoint

- **Café Grumpy · Greenpoint**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house · Settle in
  - 193 Meserole Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11222
  - Daily 7:00–18:00
  - Café Grumpy's first and roomiest café, opened in 2005 when this corner of Greenpoint was still largely Polish, with the company's roastery next door. Plenty of tables and Wi-Fi make it a place to sit for a while.

### Williamsburg

- **Devoción · Williamsburg**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house
  - 148 Grand St, Brooklyn, NY 11249
  - Weekdays 7:00–18:00, weekends 7:30–18:00
  - The Colombian roaster's Williamsburg flagship and training lab, two blocks from its original roastery café. Devoción's beans are roasted within about ten days of leaving Colombia, so the cups taste unusually fresh; buy a bag of single-origin to take home.
- **Maru Coffee · Williamsburg**: Coffee · Café · Light roast / Nordic
  - 320 Wythe Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11249
  - Daily 7:30–17:00
  - Los Angeles' Maru opened its first East Coast café here in 2025: a calm, minimalist room serving clean light roasts with pastries from Frenchette. Devoción's flagship is a couple of minutes' walk away.

### Bushwick

- **SEY Coffee**: Coffee · Café · roasting: In-house · Light roast / Nordic
  - 18 Grattan St, Brooklyn, NY 11206
  - Weekdays 7:00–17:00, weekends 8:00–17:00
  - Brooklyn's benchmark for ultra-light roasting: a bright, plant-filled warehouse with the roastery behind a glass wall. Order whichever single-producer coffee is brewing, drink it black, and buy a bag to take home; there's no tipping. Laptops are limited on weekdays and not allowed at weekends, and public cuppings run in the roastery.
- **Dayglow · Bushwick**: Coffee; Bar · Café · roasting: Guest roasters · Multi-roaster; Pour-over
  - 8 Wilson Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11237
  - Daily 7:00–23:00
  - A Los Angeles multi-roaster café with around ten pour-overs on the menu at once, from roasters rarely seen in New York, plus coffee mocktails. In the evening it becomes Niteglow, with its own brewery's beer. It's a few minutes' walk from SEY, so do both.

### DUMBO & Brooklyn Heights

- **% Arabica · DUMBO**: Coffee · Café · Espresso; Pour-over
  - 20 Old Fulton St, Brooklyn, NY 11201
  - Daily 8:00–18:00
  - The Kyoto-born café's first New York shop, under the Brooklyn Bridge with big windows facing it and counter seats along the glass. Order the Spanish latte or a single-origin pour-over, then walk out to the waterfront at Brooklyn Bridge Park.

### Flushing

- **Off-Peak Coffee**: Coffee · Stand · Grab & go
  - 38-19 Main St, Flushing, NY 11354
  - Daily 7:00–19:30 *(unverified)*
  - A narrow, minimalist takeaway counter on Main Street with Asian-leaning signature drinks: black sesame latte, pistachio latte, salted yuzu dirty, strawberry mango matcha. There are no seats, so grab one on the way into Flushing's food courts.
- **AKFamily Coffee**: Coffee · Café
  - 35-21 Farrington St, Flushing, NY 11354
  - Mon–Thu 9:00–19:00, Fri–Sat 9:00–20:00 *(unverified)*
  - A cozy neighbourhood café a few blocks off Main Street, with jazz on and a vinyl corner. Locals come for the specialty lattes, like the pistachio latte and the iced chocolate hazelnut, and for matcha; expect a wait at weekend peaks.
- **Fang Gourmet Tea**: Tea · Tasting bar; Bean shop
  - 36-60 Main St, 1st Floor Rear, Flushing, NY 11354
  - Daily 10:30–18:00
  - New York's go-to for Taiwanese tea and traditional gongfu service: sit for a tasting of rare oolongs and aged puerh, poured through several steepings by staff who explain each one. Book a tasting online at least a day ahead, and browse the teas and teaware for gifts.
