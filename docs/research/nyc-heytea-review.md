**14 Heytea shops** (the Chinese chain known for cheese-foam and fruit
teas) and **Warabimochi Kamakura** in Chelsea, from your Maps link.

- **The gelato** is at the **Gelato Lab, 863 Broadway** (Union Square):
  eight gelato and sorbet flavours made on site, opened October 2026.
- The **Upper East Side teabar** (126 East 86th St) serves Heytea's
  **tea ice cream**, which is not gelato.
- The other twelve are tea shops.

**Sources:**
- Every pin, address and set of hours comes from the shop's Google Maps
  listing, checked against OpenStreetMap where it has the shop.
- Heytea's own site has no store list, and Warabimochi Kamakura's gives
  no hours. So every row has third-party hours, and the card will badge
  them **unverified**.
