# Notes rewrite: a sample of ten (2026-11-10)

For Jerry to approve the voice before Part 2 of
`docs/proposals/content-pass.md` rewrites the other 177. Nothing here is
in the data yet. Each place shows the note before and after, what moves
into a field, and what goes to the follow-up list instead.

**The voice:** what it is, why go, what to order, and the practical tips
(booking, cash, queues, cover charges), in two or three sentences of
present tense. No hours, sources, dates of research or doubt. Short
directions stay when they help you find the door ("a minute from the
station"), since that's a tip, not an address.

**The Hours column gets the same treatment.** 67 Hours values carry
research wording too ("per Tabelog", "sources differ, verify",
"(unclear if whole store)"), some of them in older drinks rows. The
badge says "unverified" now, so the words go and the times stay.

(18:00–21:00), so confirm by phone, 03-3440-7656.

Michelin star since 2010. Very hard to book: seats open two

### Arashiyama Hanatouro (activities:4)

**Before:** Lantern-lit paths Dec 8–17, 2026, from Togetsukyo Bridge
through the Bamboo Grove to Nonomiya Shrine, the river path, and the
Jojakko-ji and Nison-in approach (~90 min). Lights from ~5pm; best
5:30–7:30. Free to walk; weeknights are calmer. JR Sagano Line to
Saga-Arashiyama (12 min). Dress warm (4–8°C); eat dinner back in
central Kyoto. Update: Address is the start of the route (Togetsukyo
Bridge); 2026 dates not independently verified.

**After:** Ten nights of lantern-lit paths, from Togetsukyo Bridge
through the Bamboo Grove to Nonomiya Shrine, along the river and up to
Jojakko-ji and Nison-in: about 90 minutes on foot, free. Come between
5:30 and 7:30 on a weeknight for the best light and thinner crowds. Take
the JR Sagano Line to Saga-Arashiyama (12 min), dress warm, and eat back
in central Kyoto.
